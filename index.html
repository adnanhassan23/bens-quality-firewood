Helpful Answers

Firewood & Delivery FAQ

Everything homeowners and cottagers ask before ordering a custom cut load.

What lengths can Ben cut my firewood?

Ben cuts directly on the yard processor anywhere from 12" up to 22" (12", 14", 16", 18", 20", or 22"). Compact wood stoves often take 12"–14", most standard home inserts take 16"–18", and deep masonry fireplaces or outdoor boilers take 20"–22".

What is the difference between a face cord and a full bush cord?

A full bush cord measures 4' high × 4' deep × 8' long (128 cu. ft. neatly stacked). A face cord is a single 4' × 8' row cut to your chosen length—at standard 16" splits, that equals roughly 42.7 cu. ft. (one-third of a full bush cord). Quarter cords (32 cu. ft.) and half cords (64 cu. ft.) are also available.

Is the wood clean, dry, and ready to burn right away?

Yes. Clean, dry hardwood that lights smoothly and burns hot is the number-one reason local families stay with Ben year after year. Seasoned stock is kept protected under tarps at the yard so it arrives ready to burn—even on last-minute weekend calls.

Can you drop close to my woodshed or garage without damaging my yard?

Yes. Ben operates a tri-axle hydraulic dump trailer and takes care to back cleanly onto driveways, pads, or right near your garage or woodshed without tearing up grass or edging. Share any tight gates or overhead branches in your order notes.

How do pricing, quotes, and payment work?

Because cut length, wood mix, and delivery town affect each haul, Ben confirms your exact price quickly by phone, text, or email before dispatching. There is no upfront online checkout—you pay upon delivery once your firewood is dropped right where you want it.

When are deliveries and yard pickups available?

Normal operating hours are Monday to Saturday, 7:00 AM – 7:00 PM, with Sunday by appointment for cottagers or urgent long-weekend runs. Call or text (905) 375-5172 anytime.
